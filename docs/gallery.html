Theme gallery

Each theme is one NAME.conf palette in dotfiles/themes, with a wallpaper from assets. theme NAME applies it live to dwm, dmenu, st, alacritty, kitty, firefox, dunst, obsidian, rmpc, vscode, emacs, claude code, neovim, fcitx5, zathura, slock, the cursor and the wallpaper, and on macOS to light/dark mode, SketchyBar, the window borders, CodeIsland and the pointer. Pick a card to see the full palette. The whole site switches to that theme too.

Fonts

Pixel fonts for the everyday look, hand-drawn ones for the soft look: font-preset pixel or font-preset bubble switches Latin and Chinese together in every app. All of them are in the assets repo with their licences, and bootstrap.sh fonts installs them.
